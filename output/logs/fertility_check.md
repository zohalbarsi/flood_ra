# Births: infoUSA vs ACS, NC and SC

infoUSA has no birth records: new children are the rise in a household's children count (CHILDRENHHCOUNT) from one year to the next. Households: primary families in the state that year that were also in the household panel the year before (step 08), with a known count in both years. Women 15-50: listed in those households (sex and age of the adult slots). Fertility rate: new children per 1,000 women 15-50. ACS rate: women 15-50 with a birth in the past 12 months per 1,000 women 15-50 (B13002, 1-year estimates; no 2020 release).
